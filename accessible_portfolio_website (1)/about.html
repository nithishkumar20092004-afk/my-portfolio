About me

Designing with people in mind

A short introduction to my skills, values and development approach.

My approach

I believe a good website should be understandable, fast and usable regardless of how someone interacts with it. I start with semantic HTML, add clear visual hierarchy, then enhance the experience with CSS and JavaScript.

Accessibility is part of the development process rather than an afterthought. I consider keyboard navigation, focus management, labels, headings, contrast, responsive layouts and reduced-motion preferences.

Skills

  • HTML5 & semantic structure
  • WCAG-oriented accessibility
  • CSS3 & responsive design
  • JavaScript fundamentals
  • SEO-friendly page structure
  • Keyboard and screen-reader considerations
